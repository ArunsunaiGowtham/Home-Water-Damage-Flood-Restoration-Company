AquaShield Policy Framework

Select below to review our concise protocols on user privacy protection or structural restoration standards of care.

1. Information Collected

We only collect data strictly necessary to dispatch emergency mitigation crews and manage property loss claims:

  • Property owner contact name, emergency phone, and address.
  • Insurance carrier details and policy claim numbers on file.
  • Live moisture sensor telemetry and psychrometric dry readings.
Data Minimization

2. Security & Encryption

We maintain enterprise-grade confidentiality standards and strict technical safeguards across our platforms:

  • 256-Bit SSL/TLS end-to-end data transmission encryption.
  • Strict zero-sale policy: no sharing with third-party advertisers.
  • Role-based access controls restricted to certified field staff.
SOC-2 Protected

3. Claims Transmission

To expedite claim approval, technical telemetry is shared solely with authorized insurance representatives:

  • Radiometric thermal inspection photos and moisture maps.
  • Standardized line-item Xactimate® mitigation billing sheets.
  • Transmitted exclusively to your designated carrier adjuster.
Direct Billing Access

4. User Privacy Rights

You retain complete transparency and control over all property restoration data stored in our systems:

  • Inspect or download complete digital copies of property records.
  • Request permanent deletion of portal accounts following job closeout.
  • Opt-out of automated SMS restoration progress notifications anytime.
User Controlled
