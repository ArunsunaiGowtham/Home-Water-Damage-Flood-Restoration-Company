What to Do in the First 60 Minutes After Sudden Water Damage

Home / Blog / Article Details
David Vance, Master Restorer October 4, 2026 5 Min Read

When a plumbing pipe ruptures, an appliance overflows, or floodwater enters your home, the clock starts ticking immediately. What you do during the first 60 minutes dictates whether the damage will cost $2,000 to dry or $50,000 in structural teardown and mold remediation.

Step 1: Eliminate Electrocution Hazards Immediately

Water conducts electricity effortlessly. If water has reached floor outlets, baseboard wiring, or has filled a basement where appliances are plugged in, do not step into standing water. Locate your electrical main breaker panel and shut off power to the affected levels if the electrical panel is safely accessible without walking through water. If you must wade through water to reach the electrical panel, call our emergency dispatch or your local utility company immediately.

Step 2: Shut Down the Main Water Supply Valve

Every adult in your household should know the location of the main water shut-off valve. In most homes, this is located where the municipal water line enters the foundation, near the water meter or inside the utility closet. Turn the valve clockwise to stop the flow of thousands of gallons into your living spaces.

Step 3: Document Everything for Your Insurance Claim

Before moving furniture or touching standing water, pull out your smartphone. Take continuous high-definition video walkthroughs and wide-angle photographs showing the water height against walls, doors, carpeting, and personal belongings. Insurance adjusters require clear visual proof of pre-mitigation conditions to approve full replacement values without disputes.

Pro Tip from Senior Restorers

Do not throw away any ruined personal belongings or cut out carpet sections before your restoration technician and insurance adjuster arrive. Everything must be inventoried on the official loss schedule.

Step 4: Lift Furniture & Draperies Above the Waterline

Wood furniture legs absorb water like sponges, transferring dark wood tannins into wet carpeting that create permanent, unremovable brown stains. Place heavy aluminum foil squares or plastic food wrap beneath furniture feet to break moisture transfer, and pin long draperies up above floor level.

Step 5: Call a Certified Emergency Restoration Specialist

Domestic fans and consumer shop vacuums cannot pull water out of subfloors, drywall cores, and insulation cavities. By calling AquaShield Pro immediately, our truck-mounted extraction fleet is dispatched within minutes, stopping toxic mold spores before the 24-hour microbial threshold.
