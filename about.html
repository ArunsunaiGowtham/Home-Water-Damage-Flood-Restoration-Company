Our Purpose

Built On Rapid Emergency Relief and Technical Precision

Founded over 18 years ago by disaster mitigation veterans, AquaShield Pro was created with a single urgent mandate: protect families and businesses from the devastating financial and health hazards of unchecked water intrusion.

When pipes burst or severe storms overwhelm municipal drainage, minutes matter. Our company maintains an active fleet of high-powered suction pump vehicles, heavy-duty LGR dehumidifiers, and thermal imaging specialists on 24/7 standby.
