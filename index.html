Hazard Awareness

Understanding Water Damage Categories

Water damage severity depends on water contamination levels. Our certified technicians deploy hospital-grade containment and psychrometric drying tailored to each category.

Category 1

Clean Water

Water originating from sanitary sources such as broken supply lines, sink overflows, or rainwater penetration.

  • Immediate extraction prevents mold
  • Degrades into Cat 2 after 24-48 hours
Learn extraction protocol →
Category 2

Grey Water

Water with significant chemical, physical, or biological contamination. Common sources include washing machines, dishwashers, and sump pump failures.

  • Antimicrobial sanitization required
  • Pad removal & deep sanitizing
View sanitation steps →
Category 3

Black Water (Hazardous)

Grossly unsanitary water containing pathogenic agents from sewage backups, rising floodwaters, or stagnant ground floods.

  • Full PPE & containment barriers
  • Mandatory porous material demolition
