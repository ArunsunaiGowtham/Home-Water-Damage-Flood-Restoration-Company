Diagnostic Accuracy

Thermal Imaging & Non-Destructive Diagnostics

You cannot dry what you cannot locate. Water naturally migrates behind wall cavities, under tile mortars, and inside insulation blankets. We use FLIR Infrared cameras to find hidden pools without tearing down drywall unnecessarily.
