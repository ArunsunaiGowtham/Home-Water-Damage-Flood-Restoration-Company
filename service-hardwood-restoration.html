Scientific Sub-Surface Hardwood Floor Moisture Extraction

Hardwood flooring is one of the most expensive architectural investments in any property. When water seeps beneath oak, walnut, maple, or engineered planks, it causes rapid swelling, expansion, and severe cupping (where edges rise above plank centers) or crowning (where plank centers bulge). If left untreated, subfloor rot and fungal mold colonization make demolition unavoidable.

AquaShield Pro utilizes commercial Injectidry Floor Mat Systems. Rather than ripping up expensive hardwood and waiting months for custom milling, our negative-pressure vacuum mats draw trapped hydrostatic vapor directly through the wood pores and subfloor plywood, restoring equilibrium moisture content (EMC) in days.

The Physics of Non-Destructive Timber Rescue

Wood is hygroscopic; it naturally absorbs liquid water until cellular saturation occurs. Our restoration engineers map exact moisture gradients using non-penetrating RF dielectric meters and hammer-probe resistance sensors. By sealing perimeter vacuum mats and operating industrial LGR desiccant dehumidifiers, we generate an extreme vapor pressure differential that forces moisture upward into our extraction stream.

Specialized Hardwood Restoration Equipment Deployed

Injectidry Floor Mat Panels

Heavy-duty clear suction panels sealed over plank seams to create continuous negative-pressure vacuum chambers.

High-CFM High-Static Blowers

Industrial negative-pressure blowers pulling up to 100 inches of water column lift from underneath tongue-and-groove boards.

Delmhorst Slide Hammer Probes

Insulated deep-probe resistance pins measuring moisture content (%MC) at both the plank surface and 3/4-inch subfloor core.

Low-Grain Desiccant Dryers

Ultra-dry heated air streams reducing surrounding relative humidity to below 20% to speed subfloor desorption.

Our 4-Stage Timber Rescue Protocol

01

Precision Moisture Matrixing

Grid-mapping electrical moisture percentages across every plank and identifying underlying plumbing leak sources.

02

Sealed Suction Mat Installation

Fastening airtight gasket-sealed drying mats over cupped zones, connected to multi-port vacuum extraction manifolds.

03

Continuous Micro-Environment Drying

Operating low-grain refrigerant systems to pull extracted vapor out of the building air envelope round the clock.

04

Final Psychrometric Certification

Confirming wood has returned within 2% to 4% of baseline dry standard, ensuring flat planks with zero mold odor.
