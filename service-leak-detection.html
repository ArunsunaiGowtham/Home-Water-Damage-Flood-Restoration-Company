Locate Concealed Moisture Before Mold Colonizes

The most dangerous water damage is often the kind you cannot see. A tiny pinhole leak in a copper pipe behind a bathroom wall, a slow condensation drip inside a ceiling plenum, or a hairline slab rupture beneath tile flooring can release gallons of moisture daily for weeks before surface staining appears. By the time water becomes visible, structural wood rot and toxic fungal growth have already spread across framing members.

AquaShield Pro utilizes state-of-the-art infrared thermal thermography, electro-acoustic listening correlators, and radio-frequency moisture impedance scanners. We find the exact source of hidden moisture within inches—eliminating destructive "guesswork" holes in your beautiful drywall and flooring.

How Infrared Thermography Works

Water-saturated building materials evaporate slowly into the ambient indoor air. This evaporative process creates a localized cooling effect called evaporative thermal dissipation. Our military-grade FLIR thermal imaging cameras detect surface temperature variances as minute as 0.05°C, instantly displaying hidden water pathways as distinct purple and dark blue cold patterns on a calibrated color screen.
