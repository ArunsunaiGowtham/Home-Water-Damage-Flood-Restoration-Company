Understanding Your Restoration Estimate

What is Xactimate® pricing?

Xactimate is the universal software used by 99% of North American insurance adjusters. Prices for labor, equipment, and materials are surveyed monthly across our metropolitan area, guaranteeing that you are never overbilled or under-reimbursed.

Why is my out-of-pocket balance $0.00?

AquaShield Pro works directly with State Farm as an accredited preferred recovery provider. Your insurance carrier pays us directly for approved line items under your Coverage A (Dwelling) policy, so you don't have to front the cash.

What happens if more water damage is discovered?

If our daily thermal imaging reveals deep structural moisture inside unexposed cavities, our IICRC lead files a Supplemental Estimate directly with your adjuster. Additional drying is approved with zero interruption to your work.

When does the reconstruction phase begin?

Reconstruction begins the moment our psychrometric telemetry confirms that framing moisture content has reached the certified Dry Standard of less than 12.0% WME (Wood Moisture Equivalent).

How is Depreciation (ACV vs RCV) calculated?

Insurance policies issue payment in two parts: Actual Cash Value (initial payment minus age depreciation) and Recoverable Depreciation. AquaShield submits final completion certificates so your carrier releases 100% of the withheld depreciation directly to our billing department.

When do I sign the Certificate of Satisfaction?

You only sign once all drying targets have been certified and reconstruction work is completed to your 100% satisfaction during our final joint walkthrough.
