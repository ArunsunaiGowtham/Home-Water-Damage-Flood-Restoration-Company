Complete Crawl Space Water Evacuation & Vapor Encapsulation

Up to 50% of the air inside your living areas rises directly from your crawl space due to the natural Stack Effect. When foundation vents allow humid air, groundwater seepage, or plumbing pipe leaks to pool in dirt crawl spaces, mold spores, rot fungi, and high relative humidity migrate directly into living rooms and bedrooms.

AquaShield Pro delivers heavy-duty crawl space encapsulation and water mitigation. We pump out standing ground water, remove damaged fiberglass insulation, treat joists with EPA-registered botanical biocides, and seal dirt floors with impenetrable 20-mil multi-ply reinforced antimicrobial vapor barriers.

Why Traditional Vented Crawl Spaces Fail

In hot and humid weather, warm outside air enters crawl space vents and condenses against cool subfloor joists and HVAC ducts. This creates continuous condensation pools that rot structural rim joists and attract wood-boring pests. Encapsulation seals off exterior humid air, turning the crawl space into a clean, dry, conditioned environment that permanently protects your property value.

Specialized Crawl Space Equipment Deployed

20-Mil Reinforced CleanSpace Poly

Multi-ply virgin polyethylene membrane infused with antimicrobial agents to resist punctures and completely block ground radon and soil vapor.

Commercial Crawl Space Dehumidifiers

Dedicated low-profile AlorAir & Quest units operating automatically at high static pressures with integrated condensate lift pumps.

Sub-soil perforated collection lines routing foundation seepage directly into dual cast-iron sealed sump basin pits.

Our 4-Stage Encapsulation Protocol

01

Debris & Water Evacuation

Clearing contaminated wet insulation, leveling soil beds, and pumping out all standing moisture.

02

Timber Mold Eradication

HEPA vacuuming wood joists and fogging non-toxic botanical disinfectants to neutralize active fungal mycelium.

03

20-Mil Thermal Encapsulation

Running continuous white vapor barriers across the entire earth floor, wrapped and sealed 12 inches up foundation walls.

04

Climate Control & Dehumidification

Installing dedicated automated dehumidification units maintaining steady 50% relative humidity year-round.
